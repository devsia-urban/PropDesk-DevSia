Why Upgrade Your Business?

Transitioning from manual processes to an automated digital system saves time, stops leakages, and scales your business.

The Current Manual Way

Right now, most of the crucial work is done manually which consumes time and leads to errors.

  • Managing Excel files & Registers
  • Relying heavily on WhatsApp & Phone Calls
  • Missing payment collections occasionally
  • Forgetting leads & follow-ups
  • Complex & error-prone manual commission calculations

The Software Solution

Everything will be solved and managed from one central unified platform.

  • 100% Paperless & Cloud-based data
  • Automated lead capture & reminders
  • Instant & accurate installment tracking
  • Never miss a client follow-up again
  • Auto-calculated team & hierarchy commissions

What Your Software Will Do

A comprehensive suite of modules tailored perfectly for real estate operations.

Lead Management

Whenever a customer comes from Facebook, Instagram, Website, or Direct Call, their details automatically sync into the software. No manual data entry.

Customer Management

Every customer gets a dedicated profile. Everything at one place.

  • Name, Phone, Address
  • Documents & Booking details
  • Complete Payment History

Team Management

Role-based secure access. Everyone only sees their own work.

  • Admin & Manager Logins
  • Leader & Sales Agent Portals
  • Performance Tracking

Township Management

Create unlimited projects, blocks, and roads. Visual plot status mapping.

  • Mark Available / Booked
  • Mark Reserved / Sold
  • Real-time inventory status

Booking System

End-to-end automated digital booking process.

  • Auto-generate Booking Number
  • Token & Receipt Generation
  • Auto-generate Agreements

Installment System

Track every single payment accurately. Always know what is due.

  • Paid & Remaining Amount
  • Next Installment Due Dates
  • Late Payment History tracking

Commission System

Software will automatically calculate payouts according to company rules.

  • Sales & Leader Income
  • Royal Income & Rewards
  • Zero manual calculation errors

Advanced Reports

Complete business oversight in just one click.

  • Daily & Monthly Sales
  • Collection & Pending Dues
  • Top Agent & Top Project stats
